Field note · Optimal yield at ½Kp. 205
A population grows fastest at half its carrying capacity: plenty of breeders, and food still to spare. Harvest there and it refills quickest.
Steward tip: Keep game species near ½K. Harvesting below ½K makes recovery slower and lowers population stability.

Unit 5 · Ecological Concepts · p. 205

Carrying capacity (K)

The largest population a habitat can support over time. Below K, a population with plenty of food grows; above K, it eats faster than its food regrows, and it starves or crashes back down.
K ≈ food energy available ÷ upkeep per individual
r = ((B + I) − (D + E)) ÷ N × 100
Logistic (S-curve)Exponential (J-curve)This run: overshoot
